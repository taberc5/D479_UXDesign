  • About Taniti's people

    • Taniti has an indigenous population of approximately 20,000. Prior to the rise of tourism, most of the economy was dominated by fishing and agriculture.

    Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. In rural areas, especially with older residents, very little English is spoken.

  • Currency

    • Taniti uses the U.S. dollar as its currency.

    Many businesses will also accept euros and yen.

    Several banks facilitate currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

  • Medical facilities

    • Taniti has one hospital with many multilingual employees.

    There are also several medical clinics.

  • Power adapters and chargers

    • Power outlets are 120 volts. These are the same as in the United States.

  • Holiday closures

    • Here on Taniti, we enjoy a large number of national holidays.

    Visitors should plan accordingly, though, as many tourist attractions and restaurants will be closed on holidays.

  • Alcoholic beverages

    • Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m.

    The drinking age on Taniti is 18.

    Parents, please note that the drinking age is NOT strictly enforced.

  • Criminal Activity

    • Violent crime is very rare on Taniti.

    Do be aware that as our tourism increases, there have been more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes.
