We’re very excited to introduce you to our lush, tropical island located in the Pacific. While Taniti is small in terms of area, at just under 500 square miles, the island is quite large in variety!
From lush tropical rainforests where you can zip-line to beautiful beaches of both sandy and rocky varieties, there's a lot of wonder and adventure to experience. Taniti even has a mountainous interior featuring an active volcano!
